The kit now has fourteen components: Dialog, Sheet and ConfirmDialog (from core v1.4.0's specs), and Tabs, TabBar and Sidebar (from core v1.3.0's), join the eight before. Each passes every claim its spec names on `windows-latest`. A ConfirmDialog with an `Action` words its question from the action's declared confirmation, as the consent prompt does.

**Known gaps:** a Sheet has no drag to dismiss on Windows; it closes with its Close button or Escape.

**Checked on:** CI, on `windows-latest` and `ubuntu-latest`. That is Windows Server, not a Windows 11 client (RELEASING.md).
